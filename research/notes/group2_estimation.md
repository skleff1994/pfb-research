## Answer to the (a)/(b)/(c)/(d) question
- (a) One plausible state: dominant (inverse FEM, GelSlim, optical tactile, Boussinesq inversion, intrinsic contact sensing).
- (b) Distribution over states: particle filters and GP models (Koval et al.; GP tactile surface work). These are over poses/shapes, not pressure distributions.
- (c)/(d) All consistent states and guaranteed bounds: found only in the pushing-mechanics line, namely Huang et al. 2017 (exact bounds over all pressure distributions sharing a CoP, on motion) and the older conservative bounds (Mason, Peshkin-Sanderson). In grasping, worst-case over uncertain contact position or friction exists (item 11) but for force closure with point contacts. I found no paper computing bounds on the admissible tangential-wrench (torsional friction) support function conditioned on taxel masses, nor with the unknown-CoP M_F case.
- So the answer is: information-conditioned bounds exist in robotics only for the CoP-known pushing case; taxel-conditioned bounds appear novel on this search, which was a limited web search (about 16 queries) and does not prove absence.
